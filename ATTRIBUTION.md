# Attribution

## Verb data

The verb data in `data/` and the bundled database (`app/src/main/assets/verbs.db`)
are derived from the OpenRussian.org dictionary database.

- Source: https://en.openrussian.org/dictionary
- License: Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International (CC BY-SA 4.0),
  https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/
- Changes: selection of the most frequent verbs, normalisation of stress marks,
  manual corrections listed in `data/overrides.csv`.

The derived data is distributed under the same license (CC BY-SA 4.0).
OpenRussian audio files are not used.
